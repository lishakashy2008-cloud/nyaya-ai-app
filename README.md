#  Nyaya AI - Legal Help for Everyone in India

Nyaya AI is a mobile-first web app that helps people in India understand their legal rights in simple language - in Hindi & English.

No more confusing legal language. Just clear, simple help.

##  Features

- **AI Legal Assistant** - Ask any legal question in simple Hindi / English
- **Find Nearby Lawyers** - Search lawyers by city and case type
- **Emergency Legal Help** - What to do if arrested, FIR process, women safety laws
- **Know Your Rights** - Fundamental rights explained in easy language

##  Built With

- React + Vite
- Tailwind CSS
- TypeScript
- Bolt.new
